# Ecommerce-Sales-Analysis
Training my skills in data visualization with this dataset about ecommerce sales. Employed my DAX and time intelligence functions.
📌 Project Overview
This project provides a 360-degree view of an e-commerce business's performance. By analyzing sales data, customer demographics, and product categories, the dashboard identifies key revenue drivers and areas for operational improvement.

📊 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard tracks vital business metrics including:

Total Revenue & Profit: Overall financial health at a glance.

Sales by Category: Identifying top-performing product lines (e.g., Electronics, Clothing).

Customer Segmentation: Analyzing purchase behavior by region and gender.

Order Volume: Tracking daily and monthly sales trends to identify peak shopping periods.

🔍 Strategic Business Insights
Product Performance: Discovered which product sub-categories contribute the highest profit margins versus those that drive high volume but low returns.

Geographic Trends: Identified the top-performing states/regions, allowing for targeted marketing spend.

Monthly Growth: Tracked Month-over-Month (MoM) sales fluctuations to prepare for seasonal demand.

🛠️ Technical Stack
Power BI: Advanced data visualization and interactive dashboarding.

Power Query: Data cleaning, attribute transformation, and normalizing messy sales records.

DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): Implemented to calculate Total Sales, Profit Margins, and Year-over-Year (YoY) growth metrics.
